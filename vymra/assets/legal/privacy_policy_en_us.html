Vymra Privacy Policy

Effective Date: May 26, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Vymra collects, uses, stores, and protects information when users in the United States access or use the app.

1. Information We Collect

We may collect the following categories of information:

  • Account information such as username, profile image, and account identifiers.
  • Pet information, wellness records, reminders, media, and related user-submitted content.
  • Purchase and balance records associated with in-app digital items.
  • Technical and usage data needed to operate, maintain, and improve the app.

2. Device Permissions

Vymra requests access to certain device features to provide full functionality:

Camera

We access your camera when you choose to take photos within the app, such as capturing pictures of your pet for their profile or recording meal photos for AI nutrition analysis. You can still use the app without camera access by selecting photos from your library instead.

Microphone

We access your microphone to enable voice input features, allowing you to dictate notes, ask questions, and create captions without typing. Voice input is optional, and you can use text input as an alternative.

Photo Library

We access your photo library to let you select existing photos for your pet's profile or other app features. We may also save photos created within the app (such as AI-generated images or analyzed meal photos) to your library when you choose to do so.

Speech Recognition

We use Apple's speech recognition service to convert your voice into text for chat, comments, and pet records. Audio data is processed in real-time and is not stored on our servers.

App Tracking Transparency

We may request permission to track your activity across other companies' apps and websites to improve the app experience and provide personalized pet health insights. This is optional, and the app functions fully regardless of your choice.

3. How We Use Information

We may use information to:

  • Provide and maintain Vymra features and account functionality.
  • Store and display your profile, pet, and app activity data.
  • Improve performance, reliability, usability, and product planning.
  • Respond to support inquiries, legal obligations, or safety concerns.

4. Legal Bases and Regional Intent

Vymra is intended for use in the United States. Where applicable, we process information based on your consent, our legitimate interests in operating and improving the service, performance of our agreement with you, and legal compliance requirements.

5. Sharing of Information

We do not sell your personal information. We may share information only when reasonably necessary to operate the app, comply with legal obligations, enforce rights, prevent fraud, or protect users and the service.

6. Data Storage and Retention

Information may be stored on your device and in supporting service infrastructure as needed to provide the app. We retain information for as long as reasonably necessary for the purposes described in this Policy unless a longer period is required by law.

7. Data Security

We use reasonable administrative, technical, and organizational measures to safeguard information. However, no method of transmission or storage can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

8. Your Choices and Rights

Depending on applicable law, you may have rights to access, correct, delete, or limit certain personal information. You may also update profile details directly within the app or request help through the contact email below. You can manage or revoke permissions at any time through your device settings.

9. Children's Privacy

Vymra is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 without appropriate authorization where required by law.

10. Policy Updates

We may revise this Privacy Policy from time to time. When material changes are made, we may update the effective date and provide notice through the app where appropriate.
